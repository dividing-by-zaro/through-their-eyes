The New Student

Maya clutched her schedule and stared at the crowded hallway. Everyone seemed to know exactly where they were going. Everyone except her.

It was her first day at Lincoln Middle School, and she had already gotten lost twice. The building was huge compared to her old school, and the map they gave her made no sense.

"You look lost," said a voice beside her.

Maya turned to see a girl with bright red hair and a friendly smile. "Is it that obvious?"

"A little bit. I'm Jade. What class are you looking for?"

"Room 247. Science with Mr. Patterson."

"That's my class too! Follow me—it's upstairs in the weirdest corner of the building. I got lost for a whole week when I started here."

As they walked together, Maya felt some of her nervousness fade away. Maybe this new school wouldn't be so bad after all.
